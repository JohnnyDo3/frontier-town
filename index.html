Frontier Town

Early playable build · the starting town
Lighting the lamps…
About 60 MB. Best on a desktop browser.
  • WASD Walk
  • Shift Sprint
  • Mouse Aim
  • L-Click Attack · hold to chop or mine
  • R-Click Block · tap to parry
  • Space Dodge roll
  • E Talk · use · enter
  • 1 2 3 Sword · axe · pickaxe
  • Q Eat or bandage
  • Tab Pack and skills

Start at the contract board in the plaza. Borrow tools from the yard foreman, gather timber and stone, craft a repair kit at the workshop and mend the well. Hollows roam past the yard. Click the game once so it takes your keyboard.
